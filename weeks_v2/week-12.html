Twelve weeks, one method

A1 Total Recall → A2 Evidence + Workflow Map → A3 Prototype + Verification → A4 Workflow Package

decision → evidence → comparison → action → limit

Today you show the package, and test whether a stranger (human or machine) can follow that thread.

Quick-fire

Four minutes each

24 min · 4 min talk + 2 min questions

Put your Object Card on screen and walk the thread:

  1. Decision: what was being decided, and for whom?
  2. Evidence: what workflow, what sources?
  3. Comparison: against what threshold, option or criterion?
  4. Action: what changes in the design or research?
  5. Limit: where does this stop being true?

Hard stop at 4 minutes. Volunteer first, then random order. Keep the other three on their scorecards during questions.

Questions that test a link

  • “What would have changed your recommendation?”
  • “How would I rerun this?”
  • “Which source location does that rest on?”
  • “Where does this stop being true?”

Question the claim, not the person.

While others present, note one strong move and one open question for each.

The judge

An LLM has read your Object Card

It saw only the card: not your talk, your data or your capsule.

It was told to:

  • restate your recommendation in one sentence
  • give up to four criticisms, each quoting the card
  • name one strength
  • list what it cannot judge from the card
  • give no score

The exact prompt and ground rules. Critiques were generated before class (model and date recorded); one is run live.

Round 1: did it understand you?

“Your card recommends…”

The author answers: yes · partly · no

If it got it wrong, that’s information about your card, not just about the model.

Round 2: judge each criticism

Label When the criticism…
Grounded quotes the card accurately, and the problem is real
Off-target quotes accurately, but misreads the purpose or uses the wrong standard
Invented relies on something not on the card, or misquotes it

Then: what did it miss that the room caught in the quick-fire?

Mark it on the Judge Scorecard (PDF).

The only scores today

go to the judge.

Judge’s score = grounded criticisms out of four, for each card.

Last week we asked papers to show where their evidence was. Today we ask the same of the machine.

Round 3: the author rules

5 min per card, all three rounds

For each Grounded or Off-target point:

accept · adapt · defer · reject, with a reason.

“The LLM judge said ___. The room called it ___. I ___, because ___.”

One of these goes in your ledger, with AI check as the source. The A4 brief already accepts that.

Run one card live, from a volunteer who consented, right after showing its pre-generated critique. Ask the room whether the two runs agree. If a student opted out, the instructor gives a human critique in the same format, and the room judges that critique just the same.

Break

10 min

When we come back: what the judge was good and bad at, then your final fixes.

Session 2 · Judge vs room

What did we learn about the judge?

8 min

  1. Which kind of problem did it catch best? Wording, logic, missing limits?
  2. What did it invent or misread, and why?
  3. What did the room catch that it couldn’t?
  4. Would you use it on your next project, and at which stage?

A critic you can check is useful. A critic you can’t check is just confident.

Last fixes, last ledger entries

18 min

Act on one point from today, from the room or the judge.

Then write two ledger lines:

  1. Room: “___ asked ___. I changed ___ / I didn’t, because ___.”
  2. AI check: “The judge said ___, labelled ___. I ___, because ___.”

If the change is to the capsule: rerun it and confirm the key figure still comes out.

What transfers

Round table

16 min · 4 min each · no slides

“The reasoning move I’m keeping is ___.

It worked here because ___.

Before I reuse it on another project, I’d check ___.”

PhD: the next chapter or study. Studio: the next project.

This feeds the Reflection Note. It’s a conversation, not a presentation. Ask one follow-up per person: “what would make that move fail?”

Submission check

5 min

☐ Practice Case · Research Brief · Object Card · Capsule · Ledger (≥3) · Reflection Note
☐ Every key source has a location (section, page, figure)
☐ Someone else has rerun the capsule
☐ Today’s two ledger entries are in
☐ No API keys or passwords in the capsule
